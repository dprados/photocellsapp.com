Getting good times

Where should I put the phone?

At the side of the lane, at hip height if possible, pointing across the track. Line up the red line with the crossing. Keep the background still (avoid trees moving in the wind or passing traffic right behind the line).

Sun and shade

Exposure is locked when the session starts. Avoid placing the line where the light changes a lot (from strong sun to deep shade). Before the athlete starts, check that the indicator at the top of the session shows DETECTOR ACTIVO in green.

A crossing was missed or an extra one appeared

Open the session settings and use "Discard last cut" for an extra one. Check that the phone is stable and the detector indicator is green (DETECTOR ACTIVO) before the athlete arrives.

Sessions

How do several phones work together?

Create a session on one phone and share the 4-digit code. The others tap JOIN, enter the code and choose their position. Times are synchronised through our server, so every phone needs an internet connection.

Athletes and comments

Add athletes when you create a session or later from its summary, separated by commas. In History, tap an athlete to see only their sessions. This information stays on your devices.

Subscription

What is free?

Joining a session is free. Creating sessions requires Photocells Pro (7-day free trial, then monthly or yearly).

Restore a purchase

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore, signed in with the same Apple Account or Google account you used to subscribe.

Manage or cancel

iPhone: Settings app → your name → Subscriptions. Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal.
