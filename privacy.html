Privacy policy

Short version: Photocells has no account, no ads and no tracking. The camera image never leaves your phone.

Camera

Photocells uses the camera to detect when someone crosses the line. Frames are analysed in memory on your device. To let you check each crossing, the app keeps the frame of each crossing in memory while the session and its summary are open, and discards it when you leave them. No photo or video is saved to your device, your history or iCloud, or sent anywhere.

Sessions and our server

To synchronise the phones of a session, Photocells uses our server (photocellsapp.com). It stores what a session needs to work: its type, the distances you enter, the participating devices and the crossing times. Devices are identified with a random token created by the app; it is not linked to your name, email, Apple Account or Google account. Data travels encrypted (HTTPS/WSS). You can ask us to delete the sessions of your device by writing to dpappssupport@gmail.com.

Athletes and comments

The athlete names and comments you add to a session are stored only on your device. They are never sent to our server. On iPhone, your history is copied to your private iCloud database (Apple's iCloud, under your account) so it is available on your other devices and after a reinstall; you can turn this off in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud. On Android, it is included in your phone's backup if you have backups enabled.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We don't receive your payment details; the app only checks whether a subscription is active.

What we don't do

No analytics or advertising SDKs, no tracking across apps or websites, no location, no contacts, no sale of data.

Children

Photocells is a general-audience sports tool and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.
